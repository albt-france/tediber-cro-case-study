# Sources, licences and collection dates

Every figure in this study carries one of two tags:
- **PUBLIC DATA** - read from a public source listed below, with its date;
- **SIMULATED** - generated by `src/simulate.py` (a fictional DTC bedding store modelled on public information). A simulated figure is never presented as Tediber's.
