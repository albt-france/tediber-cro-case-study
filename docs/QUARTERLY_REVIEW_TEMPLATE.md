# Quarterly CRO review - template

**Quarter:** `<Qx YYYY>` · **Audience:** E-commerce, Marketing, UX/UI, Data, Tech, CRM, Acquisition

## 1. The quarter in one slide
- Conversion per session / per user, revenue per visit (gross and net of returns), vs previous quarter and same quarter last year
- What explains the change: traffic mix, seasonality / promotions, site changes, tests shipped (in that order)

## 2. Funnel health
Step pass rates by device, channel, visitor type and category; biggest leak and its trend; page speed (lab + real users if available).

## 3. Incidents
| Incident | Detected after (days) | Cause | Cost estimate (range) | Prevention added |
|---|---|---|---|---|
Plus: alerts closed as "traffic mix" or "promotion" (not incidents), and data-quality fixes (reconciliation).

## 4. Tests
| Test | Result [95 % CI] | Guardrails | Decision | Annualised impact (range) | Learning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
Win rate, average duration, share of tests invalidated (SRM / bugs) and why.

## 5. Backlog for next quarter
Top 5 by RICE with owner and effort; ideas dropped and why; dependencies (Tech, UX, CRM).

## 6. Measurement
Tracking changes, reconciliation trend, what we still cannot measure (e.g. returns maturing, cross-device).

*Impacts are given as ranges from confidence intervals and assume the effect holds after launch; returns are
re-read once the 100-night window has passed.*
